## Errors found in the school's own sheets

The boarding sheet's Total column disagrees with its own term figures in
three rows. The errors cancel out, so the grand total still reads 41,850 and
nobody has noticed:

| Votehead | Terms add up to | Sheet says | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Tuition | 11,200 | 10,500 | −700 |
| Accommodation | 3,800 | 2,800 | −1,000 |
| Meals | 14,000 | 15,700 | +1,700 |

Ask the bursar which column is authoritative before loading. If the term
figures are right, the boarding total is right by accident; if the totals are
right, three terms are mis-stated and parents have been billed inconsistently.

## Gaps

1. **No Grade 4–6 sheet.** We have EYE, Grade 1–3 and Junior (7–9). The
   primary middle grades are missing.
2. **Boarding is not a separate charge.** The boarding sheet duplicates the
   junior day fees rather than adding a boarding premium. Confirm what a
   boarder actually pays over a day scholar.
3. **Bus maintenance is charged to everyone** at 300 per term, in every
   structure — including pupils who do not use a bus. There is no route-based
   transport fee anywhere. Since transport is the principal's headline
   request, ask whether routes are charged separately and, if so, at what
   rates.
4. **Two addresses appear.** The letterhead says Box 93–50200 Bungoma; the
   administrator's stamp reads "Myanga–Bungoma" while the requirements form
   said Napara, Kimaeti. Confirm the physical location for the website.
