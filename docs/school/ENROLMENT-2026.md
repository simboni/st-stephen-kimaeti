## The class structure, settled

Twelve classes, and **Grade 9 is the highest**:

```
EYE      Playgroup · PP1 · PP2
Primary  Grade 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5 · 6
Junior   Grade 7 · 8 · 9
```

This document, every fee sheet and every letterhead agree: there is no
Grade 10, 11 or 12. The letterhead on this return reads "Junior Secondary &
Early Years of Education Centre".

**Scope consequence.** The brief said the school runs to Grade 12, which would
have required per-learner subject enrolment for senior pathways — learners in
one class sitting different subjects — and that is the largest piece of new
development in the project. On this evidence it is not needed, and the Holy
Cross platform fits St Stephen's academic structure almost exactly. Confirm
with the principal before quoting, in case Grade 10 is planned for a future
intake rather than running now.
